Privacy notice

What this site collects, why, and how to control it. Plain terms.

This notice covers carpathcode.com, operated by Carpathcode, a studio based in Cluj-Napoca, Romania. It describes exactly what the website does — nothing more is collected than what is listed here.

Analytics — only with your consent

We use Google Analytics 4 to understand site traffic (pages viewed, broad location, device type). It loads only after you click “Accept” on the consent banner. If you decline or ignore the banner, no analytics cookies are set and no analytics data is collected.

When enabled, Google Analytics sets cookies and processes data on Google’s infrastructure with IP anonymisation on. You can change your choice any time via “Cookie settings” in the footer, or by clearing this site’s data in your browser.

Contacting us

The calls to action open your email client to message office@carpathcode.com. There are no forms on this site. When you email us, we receive whatever you choose to send and keep it only as long as needed to respond and to run any engagement that follows.

Hosting

The site is served as static files via GitHub Pages. As with any web host, GitHub may process connection metadata (such as IP address) for security and delivery. See GitHub’s own privacy documentation for detail.

Your rights (GDPR)

You can request access to, correction of, or deletion of any personal data we hold, and you can object to or withdraw consent for analytics at any time. To exercise these rights, email office@carpathcode.com. You also have the right to lodge a complaint with your data protection authority.
